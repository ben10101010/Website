Macro Keyboard

View Project

Year of Creation: 2023

While browsing through instagram, I came across a video of a tiny keyboard that had strange buttons on it. Those buttons were able to perform specific commands and It intrigued me. I decided to make my own version of it.

Macro Keyboard

×
I started by taking a trip to Microcenter. I bought a set of replacement mechanical keyboard switches, a Raspberry Pico, and a compatible kit that let me easily wire a breadboard to the Raspberry Pico. Then I soldered all of the voltage inputs on the switches together and connected it to the negative input on the Pico. Next, I soldered wires to the inputs and plugged them into the breadboard. It took me a while to figure this out since I did not know how to read signals from the switches. I had mistakenly connected the voltage inputs to ground, but once I figured it out, It was easy work from
After connecting it, I connected it to my computer and started programming. Using python and the Adafruit HID Library, I programmed the Pico to send commands based on specific key strokes. With 24 keys, I can program well over ten thousands commands. I have set up a couple of them, such as opening a specific video link, opening command prompt, turning off wifi, etc. It's fun to use and can be pretty useful at times. Recently, I added a potentiometer to change the keyboard layout. It allows me to switch between commands and ASCII. Just by turning the knob, I can now either open shortcuts or type out special characters.
